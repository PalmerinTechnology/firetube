# FireTube

A free, open-source music player for Android, Android TV and Android Auto. It plays music from YouTube, with no ads.

## What it does

- Search songs, videos and playlists, or paste a YouTube link.
- Background playback with a home-screen widget, sleep timer and radio-style autoplay.
- Synced lyrics, chapters for full albums and mixes, and your listening stats.
- Equalizer, crossfade and playback speed.
- Playlists, favorites, offline downloads and Android Auto / Chromecast.
- Works on phones, tablets, Android TV and Fire TV, in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese.
